# Tile texturemap templates

Artist-facing templates for the Isomata sub-voxel tile shapes (`half`, `ramp`,
`half-ramp`). The rigorous format reference is
[`../../docs/tile-format-spec.md`](../../docs/tile-format-spec.md); this
directory is the drawing aid.

All files here are generated by [`gen-templates.sh`](gen-templates.sh)
(`./gen-templates.sh`; needs ImageMagick 7, `magick`). Re-run it after changing
the script; do not hand-edit the PNGs.

## The six material slots

An Isomata material has **six face slots** (`src/render/materials.h`), each a
square texture. The stock convention is **16×16 px per face**
(`assets/textures/`). The slots are, with their outward normals:

| Slot | Face | Normal |
|---|---|---|
| `TOP` | top | `+Y` |
| `BOTTOM` | bottom | `-Y` |
| `NORTH` | north | `-Z` (the map's top row) |
| `SOUTH` | south | `+Z` |
| `EAST` | east | `+X` |
| `WEST` | west | `-X` |

A single file can fill all six faces, or a material can override individual
faces (see the manifest grammar in `src/render/materials.h` and
`assets/textures/materials.txt`).

## The files

For each shape there are two PNGs:

- **`<shape>-uv-sheet.png`** — the six slots in a labelled 3×2 grid (the big
  letter in each cell is the slot: `T B N S E W`), with a side panel listing
  **which slot each face of the shape samples**. Use it as the reference for
  which texture goes where.
- **`<shape>-paint.png`** — the same six slots as **blank, empty cells** at the
  real 16×16 face size (a 50×33 sheet: cells are 16×16 with a 1 px gap). Paint
  each cell, then export it as the face texture. Cell order, row-major:

  ```
  TOP     BOTTOM   NORTH
  SOUTH   EAST     WEST
  ```

  The gaps and border are guides only — export each 16×16 cell on its own.

A slot marked in **green** is the shape's top/slope surface, **purple** the
bottom, and **blue** a vertical side (a ramp's back face or one of its two
triangles).

## Shape → slot summary

**HALF** (bottom slab, surface at `y + 0.5`):
top → `TOP`; bottom → `BOTTOM`; four 0.5-high sides → `NORTH`/`SOUTH`/`EAST`/`WEST`.

**RAMP** (full-height wedge, rise `1.0`):
slope → `TOP`; bottom → `BOTTOM`; the **back face** at the tall edge → the slot
of the rise direction (`dir=north` → `NORTH`, `east` → `EAST`, `south` →
`SOUTH`, `west` → `WEST`); the two **triangles** → the two side slots
*perpendicular* to the rise. The side **opposite** the rise is unused (the low
edge is open).

**HALF_RAMP** (half-height wedge, rise `0.5`): identical slot mapping to `RAMP`,
with a 0.5-high back face and triangles tapering to zero.

## UV orientation

Face textures read **upright and unmirrored from outside the cube**. A side
face's quad runs bottom-left, bottom-right, top-right, top-left. A ramp
triangle is a degenerate quad whose rendered half samples the **bottom-left,
bottom-right, top-right** portion of its side slot (the top-left corner is not
used for that triangle). Exact per-direction corner tables and the full culling
and lighting rules are in
[`../../docs/tile-format-spec.md`](../../docs/tile-format-spec.md) §4–5.

See the committed all-sides screenshots in
[`../../docs/images/tiles/`](../../docs/images/tiles/) for what each slot looks
like in-engine.
